Live OneDrive remains configuration-dependent. MCCS expects:
- MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID
- MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID
- MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET
- ONEDRIVE_DRIVE_ID
- ONEDRIVE_ROOT_FOLDER=MCCS

Do not invent or expose these values. Your Microsoft 365 / Entra administrator needs to provide/approve them.
